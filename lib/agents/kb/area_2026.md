## Analipsi, Hersonissos

A village, not a resort strip

**Analipsi** is a quiet village on the north coast of Crete, about 25 km from Heraklion and a 7 minute drive from Hersonissos. Narrow streets, small shops and family taverns, away from the busy strip.

It is an easy base for the beaches, for Hersonissos nightlife, and for day trips such as the Minoan Palace of Knossos. Analipsi Beach is in the village, and the beaches of Anissaras, Hersonissos and Stalis are a short drive along the coast.

## An evening in Analipsi (https://www.sirtaki-tavern.gr/blog/evening-in-analipsi.html)
You have had the pool, the sun and the buffet. It is early evening, you are staying at a hotel in Analipsi, Hersonissos or Gouves, and you would like to do something that feels like Crete. Here is how we would spend the evening.

**A simple plan**
- Leave the hotel in the early evening and walk into the village
- Take your time: Analipsi is small and unhurried
- End the walk at a table in our garden, with a Cretan wine and dinner

### Start with a walk

Analipsi is a village, not a resort strip, and it is best explored slowly. Walk the streets away from the hotels and let the village set the pace. If you are staying near the sea, you can begin with a stroll along the shore before heading into the centre.

Roughly how long you walk depends on your hotel. From the hotels in Analipsi it is between 5 and 20 minutes to our door, and our Analipsi hotels guide shows the walk from each one. If you would like a peek at the village before you leave your room, you can watch it live on our web camera.

### Slow down at a table

When you reach the centre of the village, you will find us: a family taverna that has been here since 1993. There is a garden at the back, a courtyard under a pergola, and a stone-walled dining room if the evening turns cool.

Nikos and Eleni opened Sirtaki, and today their three sons run it with them. Mama Eleni still cooks the Greek classics, and we are happy to tell you about every dish. You are not in a rush here. Order a few starters, share them, and see how the evening goes.

When dinner is over, you can walk back through the quiet village, or we will call a local taxi that picks you up at the door. This works from Analipsi, Hersonissos and Gouves. If you are walking, take your phone light on the unlit stretches.
