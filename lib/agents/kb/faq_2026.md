## FAQ page: Leaving your All-Inclusive Resort? What you need to know
Page: https://www.sirtaki-tavern.gr/faq.html

**Q: Why should I leave my hotel buffet to dine at Sirtaki Tavern?**
A: Tired of standardized resort buffets? Our family-run Greek taverna, operating **since 1993** by Nikos, Eleni and our family, offers a truly authentic Cretan evening: home-cooked Greek recipes, slow-baked specialties like Kleftiko, and premium **25-day dry-aged Cretan Black Angus** steaks, in a warm garden atmosphere.

**Q: Are you a “tourist trap” or do locals eat here too?**
A: We are a genuine, family-run Greek taverna, open **since 1993** (founded by Nikos and Eleni). We serve locals, repeat visitors and travelers who appreciate fair prices, generous portions and top-quality ingredients. No hidden charges - just honest Cretan hospitality.

**Q: Is it worth paying for dinner outside when our hotel is all-inclusive?**
A: Resort buffets are convenient, but they can feel mass-produced and repetitive. One evening at Sirtaki Tavern gives you a real night out in Greece: a warm garden under the stars, authentic home-cooked recipes and premium cuts of meat that a buffet simply cannot match. It is an investment in a memorable holiday, not just a meal!

**Q: I don’t know Greek food well - can someone help us order?**
A: Don’t worry at all! Our friendly team speaks fluent English and is always happy to guide you through the menu, explain the dishes and suggest the best local specialties or sharing plates for your taste.

**Q: Do you have lighter options, or authentic Cretan dishes we won’t find in resorts?**
A: Both! Alongside traditional Cretan and Greek recipes, homemade dips and our dry-aged Black Angus, we offer fresh seafood, lighter dishes and a wide choice of vegetarian, vegan and gluten free options - a tasty change from heavy hotel food.

**Q: Is Sirtaki Tavern close to my all-inclusive hotel in Analipsi, Hersonissos or Gouves?**
A: Yes! We are right in the heart of Analipsi, a **5 to 20 minute walk** from the resort hotels, depending on where you stay (for example Insula Alba about 11 minutes, Senseana 12, Stella Palace and Stella Village 15, Stella Island 19). Staying in the wider Hersonissos or Gouves area? We are a quick 5–10 minute drive or taxi ride away. Find your hotel on our [Analipsi hotels guide](https://www.sirtaki-tavern.gr/location/location-of-analipsi-hersonissos.html#hotels) to see how close you are.

**Q: Is it safe to walk around Analipsi at night, and how do we get back?**
A: Analipsi is a safe, peaceful and friendly village, and walking to and from the nearby resort hotels is easy and pleasant. Staying further away, or simply prefer a ride after a relaxing evening with wine? Just ask our staff - we will gladly call a local taxi to pick you up right outside our door.
