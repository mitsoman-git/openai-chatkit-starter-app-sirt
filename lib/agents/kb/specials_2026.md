## Cretan Black Angus Farm

More about Black Angus farm in Crete

The Cretan Black Angus family farm begins the year 1970, the farm facilities are located in north of Messara plain, just south of village of Galia, central Crete.

In this place they started to deal professionally with the breeding and fattening of Black Angus cows, while a few years later they expanded the facilities and created in the adjacent area farms for breeding porks and chicken, at the same time they opened their own butcher service to ensure the immediate selling of their products.

Through modern but highly natural breeding methods using natural biological disinfectants such as attapulgite, zeolite and active microorganisms (EM) and based mainly on Cretan earth foods such as cereals, oilseeds, zucchini and plenty of the same grass, aim at the production of meat of exceptional nutritional and gastronomic value grafted with the taste of the Cretan land.

### What is Kleftiko Lamb?

Kleftiko is a traditional Greek way of cooking lamb slowly until it becomes very tender. The meat is wrapped and baked gently for a long time, so the flavours of the herbs, garlic and juices stay sealed in.

It is a dish built around patience rather than a heavy sauce – the slow baking does the work, and the result is soft lamb that comes away easily from the bone.
