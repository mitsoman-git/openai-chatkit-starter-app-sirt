# Events and big groups

From the events pages of our website ([Private dining & events](https://www.sirtaki-tavern.gr/events)) and the [group pre-order page](https://www.sirtaki-tavern.gr/event/).

## What we host
Our restaurant hosts birthdays, small weddings (pre-wedding or wedding dinners), anniversary parties and business dinners for **up to 32 guests**, in our quiet, traditional back yard (garden). "Your event is our personal challenge."
- **Birthday party:** up to 32 people, pre-order the food menu, birthday cake on request.
- **Small wedding:** up to 32 guests, our management team helps create the evening, wedding decoration, pre-order the food menu, champagne for the couple on request. The back yard is ideal in summer. Please contact us at least 2 weeks before.
- **Business dinner:** up to 32 people, pre-order the food menu, the quiet back yard is ideal. Please contact us at least 2 weeks before.

## How it works (3 steps)
1. **Tell us your plan:** message us on WhatsApp or email with the date, the number of guests and the occasion.
2. **Choose your menu:** pre-order the main courses from our menu (starters can be ordered on the day) and we look after the rest.
3. **Enjoy the evening:** please contact us at least two weeks before for weddings and business dinners.

## Group pre-order page (groups of 10 to 30)
For dinners of 10 or more guests we have a free tool: the organiser creates the event, shares ONE link in the group chat, and each guest picks their own dish on their phone. The kitchen receives the full list before the group arrives.
- Events are created after speaking with us: we send the organiser an access code on WhatsApp together with the link.
- Choices close **24 hours before** the event, so book at least 2 days ahead.
- Events are served between 17:30 and 22:00 (last seating 22:00).
- Why order in advance: if a group wants 10 portions of fish or 10 moussakas we need to know beforehand, otherwise we cannot guarantee availability and there may be long delays. Drinks and desserts do not need to be on the list.
- Tip for the organiser: pick the set menu for the whole table (starters and salads to share, then mains) so nobody has to collect everyone's choices.
- Cash and card are both welcome.
